On the Subject of Layer Keys

I want to break out of this abstracting pattern of layers but it's ON.

  • This module contains 4 lettered keys and a cycling number display.
  • If the number display is not cycling, you are looking at the wrong manual.
  • Take the four two-digit numbers that are cycling on the number display and concatenate them. Use this as the ruleseed for the manual.
  • For four sublevels, determine what the sublevel's maze looks like, where the starting and ending positions are, and navigate accordingly.
